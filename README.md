# ConsumerIQ – Customer Intelligence & Campaign Response Analytics

ConsumerIQ is a customer intelligence and marketing analytics project that uses transactional, behavioral, demographic, and campaign data to understand customer behavior, identify actionable customer segments, and predict campaign response.

The project combines **feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, and machine learning** to support data-driven customer targeting and marketing resource allocation.

---

## Project Objectives

- Build a customer-level analytical dataset from raw transactional and campaign data.
- Engineer meaningful behavioral and purchasing features.
- Segment customers based on purchasing behavior and engagement.
- Analyze campaign response across customer segments.
- Build a machine learning model to predict campaign response.
- Identify the key behavioral factors influencing campaign response.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable marketing recommendations.

---

## Dataset

The project works with customer-level information derived from transaction, product, coupon, campaign, and demographic data.

### Dataset Characteristics

- **2,500 customers**
- Transactional purchasing behavior
- Product and category information
- Coupon and discount behavior
- Campaign exposure and response
- Customer demographic attributes

The final customer-level dataset contains engineered behavioral, campaign, demographic, and segmentation features.
